Welcome to myArc

myArc is an open-source browser that keeps Arc's way of browsing, with no account to sign up for. Here's where to start.

The basics

  • Spaces and Profiles

    A Space holds the tabs for one part of your life, like Work or Travel. A Profile keeps your logins apart, and several Spaces can share one.

    ⌃1 to ⌃9 jump to a Space, and ⌥⌘→ moves to the next one.

    How it works: Spaces and Profiles

  • Pinned tabs and Favorites

    Press ⌘D to pin a tab to its Space. Favorites show in every Space of a Profile, and closing either one keeps its tile.

    Press ⌘D again to unpin it. To remove a Favorite, use its right-click menu.

    How it works: Pinned tabs and Favorites

  • Command bar

    ⌘T opens it for a new tab and ⌘L on the current address. Type an action like pin or split to run it, or press Tab to list only actions.

    Tab again leaves the actions list. The arrow keys move through suggestions, Enter opens one and Esc closes the bar.

  • Peek

    A link from a pinned tab or Favorite to another site opens in a card over the page. Press ⌘O to keep it as a tab.

    Esc or a click outside closes it, and ⇧⌘T brings it back.

    How it works: Peek

  • Split View

    Press ⌃⇧= to put two tabs side by side. The pair shows as one row in the sidebar and comes back after a restart.

    ⌃⇧- separates the pair again and keeps both tabs.

  • Little Arc

    Links from other apps open in a small window, and ⌥⌘N opens one from anywhere. Press ⌘O to move the page into your current Space.

    ⌥⌘O lets you pick another Space instead.

Shortcuts to learn first

  • ⌘TNew tab
  • ⌘LEdit the address
  • ⌘DPin or unpin the tab
  • ⌃1–9Switch Spaces
  • ⌥⌘NOpen Little Arc
  • ⌃⇧=Open a Split View

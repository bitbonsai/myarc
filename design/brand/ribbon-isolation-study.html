How it was made

  • Trace. Both ribbon edges traced by hand over myarc-matte.png at 1254 px, including the left curl, the front crossing sweep, the right curl with its small hole, and the seam where the front band ends over the curl.
  • Colour. Gradient stops sampled from the original along each band: front cyan → blue → violet → magenta → pink → peach; back band bright blue → deep blue → blue-violet → pale pink. A soft contact shadow under the front band restores the fold depth.
  • Reconstructed, not visible in the original. Back band segments hidden behind both A legs and under the front sweep (roughly x 350–640 and 900–1050). Everything else follows visible edges.
  • Known gaps. Flat gradients, no specular sheen or inner glow from the original render. Edge wobble on the left inner fold is from the trace, not the original.

myArc keeps Arc's model. A Space sorts your tabs by topic, and a Profile keeps your logins apart. They are separate things, and several Spaces can share one Profile.

## Spaces

A Space is a set of tabs for one part of your life, such as Work, Reading or Travel. Each Space has its own pinned tabs and temporary tabs, and a name, an icon and a color.

- To create a Space, click + at the bottom of the sidebar. Give it a name, pick an emoji or icon, choose its Profile and pick a theme color.
- To switch Spaces, click the Space's dot at the bottom of the sidebar.
- To rename the current Space, click its name at the top of the sidebar.

## Profiles

A Profile holds your cookies, logins, history and extensions. Spaces that share a Profile share all of that, plus the Profile's Favorites. A Work Profile and a Personal Profile let you stay signed in to two accounts on the same site.

Each myArc Profile is a full Chromium Profile, so the separation is the same one Chrome gives you. To make a new Profile, open the Profile menu while you create a Space and choose New Profile.

Switching to a Space in another Profile brings in that Profile's window at the same position and size, so it still feels like one window.

## Pinned tabs and Favorites

Pinned tabs and Favorites both appear as favicon tiles at the top of the sidebar, the Favorites first.

- A pinned tab belongs to one Space and shows only there.
- A Favorite belongs to the Profile and shows in every Space of that Profile.

Right-click a tab in the sidebar to pin it to the Space or add it to the Profile's Favorites. The same menu moves a tile between the two, and Unpin from Space or Remove Favorite takes it away.

Closing a pinned tab or a Favorite keeps its tile. The page closes, and clicking the tile opens it again.

## Folders

A folder groups pinned tabs inside a Space. To make one, right-click the Space name at the top of the sidebar and choose New Folder. Right-click a folder to rename or delete it, and click it to open or close it. To file a tab, right-click it and choose Move to Folder. Remove from Folder takes it out again. Folders don't nest.

## Temporary tabs

Every other tab is a temporary tab. Temporary tabs sit in a list below the Space name and the New Tab row. They come back after you quit and reopen myArc.

When you close a temporary tab, it moves to Archived Tabs in the Library. Open the Library with the archive icon at the bottom left of the sidebar. It also lists your Spaces and recent downloads.

## Not in myArc yet

Folders inside folders and dragging a tab up to pin it don't work yet. [Known limitations](../known-limitations/) has the full list.

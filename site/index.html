How you browse is part of how you work. It should outlast the company behind your browser.

Arc is being sunsetted, and its code was never open, so nobody can keep it going. myArc is open source, so anyone can.

What we hold to

  • Yours to keep

    Your workspace stays on your computer, in files you own. Export is on the way.

  • Keep what worked

    If you used Arc, you already know myArc. Where they differ, onboarding shows you.

  • AI by choice

    AI is off until you turn it on. Then you pick: a local model, your own API key, or a hosted option, and you decide what it can see.

  • Built in the open

    The plans and decisions are public too, not just the code. If I stop, anyone can pick it up where I left off.

What works today

  • Browsing, with Chromium extensions
  • Spaces: create, switch, rename and reorder them
  • Pinned tabs and Favorites, kept across restarts
  • The Library: Downloads, Archived Tabs and your Spaces
  • Command bar Cmd+L and Arc's shortcuts
  • Hide the sidebar with Cmd+S, peek it from the window edge
  • AI agents browse in their own Agent Space, after you allow them

Not yet: split view, Little Arc, Arc import, ad blocking.

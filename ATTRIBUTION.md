# Attribution

The planning documents and documentation-check script were created for myarc. They contain no copied Arc or Artcraft source code, private standards, or user exports.

`references/arc-archive/` contains a research-only capture of the public Arc website from the [Internet Archive snapshot dated 2025-12-10](https://web.archive.org/web/20251210052636/https://arc.net/). It includes archived HTML, CSS, JavaScript, fonts, images, videos, screenshots, and a local viewing mirror. Arc, its branding, and the captured site assets remain the property of their respective owners. These files are references, not myarc product assets.

Public Artcraft repository conventions informed the development methodology. Primary-source links and limits are recorded in [docs/RESEARCH.md](docs/RESEARCH.md). This is an independently written adaptation, not a copy of their instructions or a reproduction of private shared standards.

`design/brand/assets/myarc-matte.png` and `myarc-glass.png` are user-supplied originals used unchanged in the requested identity draft. Their author, creation method and license were not supplied; redistribution and trademark clearance remain unverified. Generated raster variants and compositions inherit that limitation. No archived Arc artwork is used in the kit.

`design/brand/assets/Manrope.ttf` is Manrope, authored by Mikhail Sharanda and contributors, sourced from [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope). Its SIL Open Font License 1.1 notice is retained in `design/brand/assets/Manrope-OFL.txt`. The font file's SHA-256 is recorded in the kit's `assets/SHA256SUMS` alongside the original logo hashes.

`design/onboarding/` contains original HTML/CSS choreography, invented sample content and locally recorded synthetic-study evidence. It loads no Arc or brand-kit artwork and distributes no font files. Browser checks use separately installed agent-browser and Chrome for Testing; those tools are not bundled. The Chromium feasibility checkout is also external; `browser/upstream.json` records its exact source/tooling revisions. Full upstream dependency/license inventory remains a foundation acceptance requirement.

Future third-party additions must record path, title/component, author, source URL, exact revision/version, license, and retained notice location. Repository publication does not grant rights to unrelated product names or branding.

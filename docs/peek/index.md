# Peek

How links from pinned tabs and Favorites open in a preview over the page, and how to keep, split, reopen or turn off that preview.

Peek keeps your pinned tabs where you left them. When you click a link in a pinned tab or a Favorite that goes to another site, the page opens in a card over the current one instead of replacing it. The card grows out of the link you clicked and shrinks back when you close it. With Reduce Motion turned on in macOS, it appears and goes without the animation.

Links to the same site still open in the pinned tab, and links from your temporary tabs open as they always do. Cmd-click a link to open it in a new tab instead.

## Using the card

As in Arc, the page fills the card, and the rest of the page area dims behind it. Three round buttons sit beside the card's top right corner:

- The X closes the card. Esc, Cmd\+W or a click outside the card close it too.
- The arrows turn the page into a new tab in Today, with its history. Cmd\+O does the same.
- The split button opens the page in a Split View. A copy of your pinned page goes on the left and the Peek page on the right, as one new split in Today, and the pinned tab stays where it was.

## Bringing a Peek back

If you close a Peek by mistake, press Cmd\+Shift\+T while the same tab is showing. Only the last Peek you closed comes back, and only when you closed it yourself. If you closed a tab after it, Cmd\+Shift\+T reopens that tab first.

## Turning Peek off

Open Settings with Cmd\+, go to Links and turn off Open a Peek window when clicking on links to other sites, the same switch Arc has there. Links from pinned tabs and Favorites to other sites then open in a new tab, and the pinned page stays where it was.

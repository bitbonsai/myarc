## 2026-11-13: Peek opens cross-site links from Pins and Favorites in a card

- myArc had the Peek routing rule in `crates/workspace/src/peek.rs` but nothing on screen. Arc's archived Peek article (the [clone backlog](../.plans/2026-10-26-arc-clone-backlog.md) Peek section, its January 2024 copy and screenshots) shows a rounded card over the dimmed page with round X and expand-arrow buttons beside its top right corner, a third Split View button, Cmd+O to open it as a tab and a click outside, X or Cmd+W to close it. Rust's new `peek_action` maps each control to close or open in tab: X, Esc, Cmd+W and a press outside close it, leaving the tab strip and workspace alone, and the expand button or Cmd+O moves the WebContents with its history into the tab strip as one new Today tab of the current Space. Showing another tab or Space closes it. New Split's blank pane now keeps the sidebar field empty and its row reads New Tab, the New Tab row's label.

## 2026-11-06: Page actions settle in about 250 ms on a quiet page
